1. Permutations (nPr) vs Combinations (nCr)

4

The total pool of unique items available.

2

How many items we pick from the total.

Key Concept:
  • Combinations: Order does not matter. Choosing {A, B} is the same as {B, A}.
  • Permutations: Order does matter. AB and BA are two different permutations.

Combinations (nCr) Order doesn't matter

nCr = n!r!(n-r)!
6 possible groups

Permutations (nPr) Order matters

nPr = n!(n-r)!
12 possible arrangements
